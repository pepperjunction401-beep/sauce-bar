Welcome Aboard

Create your Pepper Junction account to collect your earned badges, track your progress, and find your way back to your favorite flavors. Already riding with us? Sign in.

Dev preview: This is the approved account-page design. Account creation, sign-in, email/phone verification, and Square purchase recovery are not connected yet. No personal information is submitted or saved here.
Adding and verifying your phone number helps us locate past purchases made through our website and at our pop-ups, and identify your existing loyalty membership. Once those purchases are verified, Pepper Junction can use your purchase history to award eligible badges and Pepper Junction Loyalty Points. You can create an account without a phone number, but some previous purchases may not be recoverable through email alone.
After you verify your account, Pepper Junction will check for safely matchable Square purchase history. No match? Your account is still yours, and you're ready for future purchases.

Password reset will be available when Supabase authentication is activated.
