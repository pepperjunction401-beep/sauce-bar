Damaged Products
What to Do If Your Order Arrives Damaged
If your order arrives damaged, don't worry — we'll make it right. Please follow these steps immediately upon receipt:
Step 1 Photograph the damaged item AND the shipping box before doing anything else. Photos are required to file a claim with the shipping carrier.
Step 2 Email your photos to pepperjunction401@gmail.com with your order details.
Window Please contact us within 14 days of delivery. Claims submitted after 14 days may not be eligible for replacement or refund.

The photos of both the product AND the shipping box are critical — carriers require both to process a damage claim. Reach out as soon as possible and we'll take care of you.
