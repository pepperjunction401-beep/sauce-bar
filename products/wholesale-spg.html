Product Overview

Pepper Junction Competition Dry Rub is a competition-style SPG seasoning developed with guidance from a Texas barbecue team. Built around sea salt, cracked black pepper, and bold garlic, it delivers the familiar foundation of traditional Texas barbecue with the balance, consistency, and visual performance expected on the competition circuit.

The coarse granules create excellent coverage and help develop a flavorful, well-defined bark on brisket, ribs, pork butts, beef roasts, and other smoked meats. Its straightforward savory profile also makes it an adaptable kitchen workhorse for steaks, burgers, tuna, roasted vegetables, soups, sauces, and seasoning bases.

Designed to perform consistently across smokers, pellet grills, charcoal pits, gas grills, and cast-iron cooking, this blend gives commercial kitchens a dependable seasoning without the variation, measuring, and preparation required by an in-house SPG recipe.

Culinary Performance

Apply evenly and allow meats to rest for 30–60 minutes before cooking. For brisket and other large cuts, season generously and refrigerate overnight when production schedules allow. The blend pairs especially well with oak, hickory, charcoal, and traditional smoke profiles.
