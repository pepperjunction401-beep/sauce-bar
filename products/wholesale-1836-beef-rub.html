Product Overview

1836 Beef Rub is an authentic Texas-style barbecue seasoning built around the classic salt, cracked black pepper, and garlic foundation that defines traditional Lone Star beef barbecue. The cornerstone of the blend is its mix of half- and quarter-cracked peppercorns, which provides bold seasoning, visible texture, and the foundation for a hardy, well-developed bark during long, low-and-slow cooks.

Named in honor of Texas declaring independence in 1836, this blend stays true to the straightforward barbecue tradition where quality beef, smoke, seasoning, and time do the heavy lifting. It is especially well suited for brisket, beef roasts, prime rib, steaks, and other cuts where a strong cracked-pepper profile is meant to complement rather than mask the natural flavor of the meat.

Its clean, savory construction makes 1836 a dependable commercial-kitchen seasoning for operators looking to achieve authentic Texas-style results without added sweetness or unnecessary complexity.

Culinary Performance

Apply 1836 Beef Rub generously to brisket and large beef cuts prior to cooking. For deeper seasoning penetration, season and refrigerate overnight when production schedules allow.

Smoke low and slow over oak or post oak and allow the bark to fully develop before wrapping. The coarse cracked pepper structure is especially effective in long cooking environments where smoke, rendered fat, and seasoning combine to create a pronounced exterior crust.

1836 also performs well on beef roasts, prime rib, and steaks, including high-heat cast-iron applications where the cracked pepper and garlic can develop a bold savory exterior.

For menu development, pair with roasted root vegetables or smoked baked potatoes. Prime rib can be finished with herb butter for added richness.
