What It Is

If you love Mikey V's Jalapeño Dill Pickle — the milder version — but need serious heat, this one turns the dial way up. It opens with that same bright dill pickle punch — tangy vinegar, garlic, and jalapeño delivering the savory brine flavor pickle lovers recognize right away. Then the Carolina Reaper kicks in and changes the experience entirely, bringing a deep, lasting heat while the classic pickle flavor stays front and center. Fantastic on burgers, hot dogs, fried chicken, or anything that welcomes a bold dill pickle bite.

Culinary Performance Notes

This sauce is built for bold finishing applications where acidity and heat can elevate rich or creamy foods. Use as a finishing drizzle rather than cooking sauce. Pair with fatty foods to balance heat intensity. Stir into dips or sandwich spreads for layered flavor. Best applied after cooking to preserve its bright dill character.

Pepper Junction Pairing Notes
  • Pour over cream cheese and serve with crackers
  • Add to chopped pickles for elevated burger topping
  • Beverage pairing: Crisp lager, hard seltzer, or off-dry Riesling
