Product Overview

Cluckerdust is a nationally award-winning chipotle barbecue dry rub crafted specifically for chicken and turkey. Built on a foundation of turbinado sugar and brown sugar, it delivers the sweetness needed for rich caramelization and flavorful skin development, followed by a smooth, family-friendly layer of chipotle warmth.

Designed to perform across grilling, smoking, roasting, and air-frying applications, Cluckerdust elevates poultry without overpowering its natural flavor. It produces dependable color, balanced seasoning, and layered sweet heat on whole chickens, wings, thighs, drumsticks, turkey breasts, and smoked turkey legs.

As Pepper Junction’s number-one-selling seasoning, Cluckerdust has earned the trust of everyday indoor kitchen heroes, backyard pitmasters, and serious barbecue cooks alike. Its nationally award-winning performance and broad menu versatility make it a dependable commercial-kitchen staple for poultry, pork tenderloin, fries, burgers, and grilled vegetables.

Culinary Performance

For even distribution and improved seasoning adhesion, lightly coat the protein with a suitable binder before applying Cluckerdust generously. Allow the seasoned meat to rest for 30–60 minutes before cooking. For deeper flavor, season and refrigerate overnight when production schedules allow.

For maximum skin crispness, cook poultry over indirect heat before finishing at a higher temperature. Monitor direct-flame exposure carefully because the sugar-forward blend will caramelize readily.

Cluckerdust performs especially well on smoked or roasted whole chicken, grilled or air-fried thighs and drumsticks, crispy wings, roasted turkey breast, spatchcock chicken, and smoked turkey legs. It pairs particularly well with applewood, cherry wood, and other mild hardwood smoke.

A lighter application also adds sweet chipotle flavor to pork tenderloin, burgers, fries, roasted potatoes, and grilled vegetables.
