What It Is

This tropical style hot sauce starts with rolling fruit from peaches, pineapple, papaya and mango, then the habanero heat rises behind it and settles in for a nice slow burn. The balance keeps the sweetness lively while the spice builds just enough to keep things interesting. This versatile sauce is fantastic with tacos, rice, eggs, chicken, oysters, fish, vegetables — even a bagel with cream cheese if you're feeling adventurous.

Culinary Performance Notes

Tears of the Sun performs best where sweetness can contrast savory or smoky elements. Use as a flavor enhancer for fresh made salsa for tacos or salmon or sea bass. Stir into sour cream for a tropical heat dip. Splash onto a bagel with cream cheese for lox and smoked salmon. Pairs especially well with charcoal grilling and fruitwood smoke.

Pepper Junction Pairing Notes
  • Pair with grilled shrimp and mango salsa for a bright summer plate
  • Add to pork tacos with pineapple slaw
  • Brush lightly over wings before serving
  • Beverage pairing: Tropical IPA, mojito, or chilled rosé
Ingredient Transparency

Peaches, pineapple, papaya, mango, habanero peppers, light brown sugar, lime and garlic.
